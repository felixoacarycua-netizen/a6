Privacy Policy & Client Data Governance

Last Updated: April 2026 | Effective Date: Immediate

1. Institutional Overview & Data Stewardship Commitment

Shearling Tote operates as an independent luxury maroquinerie atelier and bespoke design salon dedicated to handcrafted shearling carryalls and heirloom leathercraft. We recognize that transparent data governance is an essential facet of discreet client hospitality, safeguarding patron trust with the same uncompromising integrity applied across our leather crafting benches. This Privacy Policy governs all bespoke commission registrations, private salon appointments, newsletter enrollments, and informational inquiries submitted across our online platforms.

2. Categories of Personal and Commission Information Collected

When patrons interact with our digital atelier, we collect identifying contact credentials including full legal names, personal electronic mail addresses, telephone coordinates, and physical delivery addresses. We also record bespoke design specifications, personalized monogram initials, leather color preferences, and consultation appointment schedules necessary to execute tailored carryall commissions safely. All communications and private commission requests are archived systematically within encrypted databases to maintain seamless salon service standards.

3. Methods of Data Collection and Authorization Consent

Client data is gathered directly through electronic consultation forms, private salon booking questionnaires, and authenticated digital correspondence initiated voluntarily by patrons seeking bespoke commissions. In addition, our web infrastructure records basic technical telemetric data such as browser specifications, operating system versions, and anonymized access timestamps via standard server logs. By submitting booking requests, patrons provide explicit authorization for our maroquinerie team to process their information strictly for commission facilitation.

4. Purposes of Information Processing and Atelier Application

All personal details collected are processed exclusively to manage private salon consultations, schedule bespoke handcrafting timelines, coordinate secure insured delivery, and deliver urgent order status notices. We utilize verified contact coordinates to confirm commission specifications, review leather hide selections with master artisans, and distribute technical maroquinerie research monographs. Shearling Tote strictly prohibits the commercial sale, leasing, or unapproved transfer of personal consumer records to third-party marketing entities.

5. Data Retention Protocols, Storage Security, and Encryption

We maintain rigorous technical, administrative, and physical security safeguards to defend collected patron profiles against unauthorized inspection, data alteration, destructive breach, or illicit extraction. Encrypted storage vaults and modern transport layer security protocols safeguard all digital transmissions between your web browser and our internal servers. Patron profiles are retained strictly for the duration required to satisfy lifetime craftsmanship warranties and legal accounting compliance, after which they are securely expunged.

6. Patron Data Rights, Contact Channels, and Legal Inquiries

Patrons possess comprehensive legal rights to inspect, amend, rectify, or demand the permanent deletion of their personal information stored within our atelier registry at any time. To exercise these individual privacy privileges or request clarifications regarding our data management practices, please contact our institutional compliance office directly. Inquiries should be directed to Shearling Tote Atelier, located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or via direct telephone at +1-888-777-5845.
