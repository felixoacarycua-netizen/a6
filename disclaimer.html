1. General Educational and Maroquinerie Scope

The leathercraft monographs, material science treatises, tanning chemical composition tables, and care tutorials presented across the Shearling Tote website are published strictly for cultural, educational, and collector enrichment purposes. Nothing published on this platform constitutes professional chemical engineering, industrial manufacturing, or commercial leather-brokerage counseling, nor should it substitute for direct consultations with master artisans. Readers must exercise independent judgment before implementing specialized home leather restoration procedures.

2. Natural Material Characteristics and Handcraft Tolerances

Because our tote bags are individually handcrafted from biological Alpine Merino sheepskin pelts and slow-tanned Tuscan hides, dimensions may vary slightly within a tight handcraft tolerance of three millimeters. Furthermore, natural skin neck wrinkles, subtle color striations, and variable fleece curl formations are innate characteristics of genuine full-grain leather goods. Shearling Tote cannot guarantee exact geometric homogeneity across individual carryalls, as each finished piece represents an unrepeatable artisanal creation.

3. Absence of Chemical Performance Guarantees

While natural lanolin and vegetable-tanned vachetta leather provide organic water resistance against light snowflakes and autumn mist, our carryalls are not sold as synthetic waterproof technical diving equipment. We explicitly disclaim any assertion that untreated luxury suede will remain impervious to permanent staining if subjected to saturated rains, corrosive salt slushes, or oily cosmetic spills. Patrons are urged to exercise sensible stewardship when carrying delicate natural shearling through adverse weather.

4. Strict Proscription Against Counterfeit Reproduction

Shearling Tote enforces an uncompromising zero-tolerance policy regarding counterfeit imitation, unauthorized commercial rebranding, and grey-market replication of our registered tote designs and trademark appointments. Our atelier does not authenticate, inspect, or warrant items acquired through unverified third-party internet resellers, auction brokers, or secondary auction portals.
