Cookie & Tracking Technology Policy

Last Updated: April 2026 | Effective Date: Immediate

1. Essence and Purpose of Digital Cookies

Cookies are small textual data fragments transmitted to your device hardware when you access modern web environments, enabling servers to recognize unique sessions and preserve essential navigational preferences. At Shearling Tote, we utilize minimal, privacy-centric cookie architectures designed to ensure website stability, simplify bespoke booking pathways, and monitor overall platform efficiency. This Cookie Policy explains the precise nature of these digital files, their functional deployment, and your sovereign authority to manage them.

2. Strictly Necessary and Operational Session Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are vital for the technical execution of our core website services, permitting patrons to move seamlessly across reservation interfaces and secure salon inquiry forms. These transient data tokens expire immediately upon termination of your active browser session and do not store enduring personal identification markers on your computer. Disabling these essential technical files through customized browser overrides may impair page navigation and prevent the submission of private dining appointments.

3. Analytical and Performance Measurement Cookies

We deploy first-party analytical telemetry, including Google Analytics cookies, to comprehend how visitors discover, navigate, and engage with our published leathercraft monographs and carryall collections over time. These performance cookies aggregate anonymous statistical metrics, measuring visitor counts, average reading durations, and technical load latencies across desktop and mobile devices. These collective insights empower our design team to optimize website readability, refine layout responsiveness, and improve reader engagement without tracking personal identities.

4. Preference and Functional Customization Tokens

Functional cookies enable our platform to recall custom preferences designated during previous visits, including preferred currency formatting, language parameters, and localized dining service selections. By preserving these user-selected attributes, our digital portal delivers an elevated, frictionless guest experience that mirrors the attentive hospitality practiced within our dining salons. These persistent functional tokens remain housed safely on your device hardware until their scheduled expiration or manual deletion.

5. User Governance, Browser Controls, and Opt-Out Protocols

Visitors maintain absolute discretion over the authorization, configuration, and deletion of digital cookies across their preferred web browsing software at all times. Most contemporary web browsers provide direct privacy settings that allow you to reject all cookies, accept solely first-party tokens, or trigger instant alerts whenever cookies are transmitted. Modifying your cookie settings can be executed effortlessly within the privacy or preferences panels of browsers such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.

6. Policy Revisions, Institutional Address, and Contact

Shearling Tote reserves the right to amend, update, or recalibrate this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect emerging technological standards, legal requirements, or adjustments to our reservation systems. Guests are encouraged to revisit this page intermittently to remain fully apprised of our tracking standards and privacy commitments.
