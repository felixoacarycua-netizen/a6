Frequently Asked Questions

Authoritative recommendations on natural shearling maintenance, rain protection, vegetable leather conditioning, and bespoke ordering timelines.

How does natural shearling perform in rain, snow, and damp winter weather?
Natural sheepskin wool fibers are coated with lanolin, an organic wax that makes the fleece naturally water-repellent. Light snow and rain bead up effortlessly on the wool surface. If your tote gets wet, simply shake off excess moisture and allow it to dry naturally at room temperature away from radiators or direct heat.
What is the difference between genuine shearling and faux synthetic sherpa?
Genuine shearling is a single natural pelt where the wool fur remains biologically attached to its original lambskin leather hide. It breathes naturally, regulates temperature, and lasts for decades. Synthetic sherpa is made from polyester plastic fibers glued to a backing that pills, sheds microplastics, and deteriorates rapidly.
How do I clean and maintain the curly wool fleece over time?
Routine care is remarkably simple: gently brush the fleece with a dedicated wooden sheepskin wire slicker brush to untangle wool fibers and remove dry surface dust. For localized spots, dab with a damp cloth and mild wool-safe detergent. Never dry-clean with harsh petroleum solvents.
Why does vegetable-tanned leather darken and develop a rich patina?
Vegetable tanning utilizes natural tree barks and plant polyphenols that react organically to ambient sunlight, air oxidation, and the natural oils from your hands. Over months of use, pale vachetta leather deepens into an exquisite golden honey and warm cognac patina unique to your journey.
How long does a bespoke hand-stitched commission take to produce?
Because every tote is hand-cut and saddle-stitched by a single artisan without mass production machinery, lead times typically range between four and six weeks from pelt selection and design sign-off at our 181 Mercer Street salon.
Can my shearling tote be sent back to the atelier for refurbishment?
Yes. All Shearling Tote pieces carry our lifetime atelier care guarantee. Patrons may return their carryall for professional edge re-waxing, wool deep conditioning, leather nourishment, and seam maintenance at any time.
